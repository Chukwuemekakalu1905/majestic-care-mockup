About the homes

A quieter place to stabilize and rebuild routine

Majestic Care LLC is a licensed Arizona Behavioral Health Residential Facility serving adults at two East Valley locations: Tempe and Gilbert. Care is residential and available around the clock.

The homes are overseen by administrator Stephen Iwuozo. Public listings describe support for adults, including those experiencing depression and anxiety, in a setting meant to feel closer to a residence than an institution.

One 2024 review called the facility “delightfully clean and well staffed” and “very responsive to client needs.”
